Ask a gardening question, share an observation, or help someone else make sense of what is happening in their garden.

Browse before you post

Open Community to read discussions. Use the sort menu for Latest, Helpfulness, Most active, or Unanswered. In Search → Community, search across All, Posts, Replies, or Profiles, and browse topics to narrow your interest.

Replies in search are grouped under the discussion they belong to, so you can open the conversation for context.

Set up your public profile

Open the Community menu and choose Community account to sign in with Apple. Use Your profile to see your contributions and edit your display name and optional bio.

Your public profile helps other gardeners understand who is contributing. Your private garden plans and journal are not automatically published with it.

Start a discussion

Use New discussion to write a clear title, choose a topic, and describe what you want to ask or share. Add relevant plants and a photo when they add useful context to your post.

Useful context might include how long the issue has been happening, what changed recently, or what you have already tried.

Before submitting content for the first time, review the safety-check permission. Community uses automated checks on submitted text and photos. You can decline and keep browsing, or change the permission later in Settings. Turning permission off does not remove posts you already shared.

Tap Post when you are ready to share publicly.

Reply with the right context

Open a discussion and choose Join the conversation, or use Reply on a particular reply. The composer identifies the discussion or gardener you are replying to.

Expand the composer when you need more room, and show the full post or reply context when you want to refer to it while writing. Use the formatting control for emphasis, or add a photo where offered. Tap the send button when your reply is ready.

Recognize useful contributions

Use Helpful on a discussion or reply that helped you. Tap it again to remove your mark. You cannot mark your own contribution helpful.

A helpful mark recognizes someone’s contribution; it is not a guarantee that gardening advice applies to every plant, location, or situation. Open a gardener’s name to explore their public discussions and replies.

Report or block

Use the more options (…) menu on a contribution to Report it or Block its author. Reporting sends it for review; the gardener is not shown who reported it.

Blocking hides that gardener’s posts and replies while you are signed in and prevents replies between you. You can review blocked gardeners from the Community menu.
