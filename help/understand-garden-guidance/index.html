Understand your garden’s guidance

7 min read

Use the Guide to understand your planting choices and notice what may need attention as your bed or containers change.

Guidance for each stage of your plant’s lifecycle

With nothing selected, guidance looks across the bed or container group. Select a plant to focus on that planting. Guidance helps you plot and plan before planting. As plants grow, it brings attention to establishment, support, weather, and seasonal changes.

The Guide shows relevant advice for what you are doing and how far along your plants are.

Start with Today when you want the wider view

Almanac → Today gathers attention items, upcoming plantings, active schedule windows, and recent journal entries across your garden. Plan plotted plants brings together plants that still need a method and date, organized by growing space. Open a relevant planting or growing space for its detailed Guide.

What shapes the advice?

Plotwise uses the plant’s details, your chosen planting method and date, location-based climate and weather, sunlight, and the plant’s stage in its lifecycle to provide guidance. The chosen variety and available space also help shape the advice.

Get to know the Guide sections

The sections change with your selection, planting details, and growing conditions. Here is what each one helps you understand.

About

A short introduction to the plant while you’re plotting it. Tap the heading to open its plant information.

Plotted, Planned, and Planted

When a planting is selected, this section shows its progress. Plotted means you’ve chosen its space. Planned means you’ve saved a planting method and date. Planted means you’ve confirmed when it began growing there.

Use Edit plan to choose or change the planned method and date. Once the plan is saved, use Plant to record the actual planting. Estimated timing appears here when available, including the kind of growing window that applies to the plant.

Planting

Practical guidance before planting, such as spacing, seed depth, or how the plant is usually started. The advice can change with the chosen planting method.

Layout

An overview of occupied and available space. In a bed, it describes the squares in use and larger planting footprints. In a container group, it summarizes how the containers are being used.

Space & growth

Guidance about mature size, spreading or climbing growth, and supports to prepare or maintain. In containers, it can also flag plants that may need more room.

Follow Explore supports when offered. Support guides in Garden Supplies explain options such as stakes, cages, and trellises and help you choose one suited to your plants.

Sun

How the available sunlight suits your plants. In beds, it can also describe shade that taller plants may cast on their neighbors. While plotting, it helps you choose a spot; after plotting, it can highlight a poor light fit or a shading concern.

Climate

How your location, planting method, and date fit the season. Before planting, this can include suggested dates or a warning about seasonal timing. After planting, advice focuses on the conditions your plants may face, such as cold or heat.

Moisture

Watering considerations based on the plants and available weather information. Advice may call attention to a dry stretch, recent rain, heavy rain ahead, or containers that can dry out quickly.

Companions

Relationships between neighboring plants, including potential benefits and combinations that need attention. While plotting in a bed, you can highlight related plants to help choose their positions. See the companion instructions below.

Health

Common pests, diseases, disorders, and symptoms worth checking for the plants you’re growing.

Follow Explore health guides when offered. The Garden Health guide is a reference to help you prepare for or investigate health-related issues that may arise in your garden.

Growth

Changes in how a plant develops, such as cool-season greens starting to bolt in warm conditions. This advice can help you decide what to watch for or harvest as the plant changes.

Harvest

Harvest timing and signs to check on the plant itself, such as the size or appearance of the part you’ll pick. Depending on the plant and its stage, it can describe an upcoming window, current production, or what to check after an estimated window has passed.

Succession

Guidance for growing another planting in the same space. Across a bed or container group, it can describe differences in when plantings are expected to finish. For a selected planting with a suitable timing estimate, it offers controls to add and review the next plantings.

Follow Plan what grows next for the full walkthrough.

Tap an explanatory guidance heading for more detail about that section.

Extra guidance for containers

Select a container to see its plantings and available room.

  • Space: General guidance about choosing containers with enough room for roots and moisture, shown when the group is empty.
  • Open spaces: How many planting spaces remain available in the selected container.
  • Container size: A warning when a planting may need a larger container for strong root growth.
  • Won’t fit: An explanation when an attempted planting cannot fit in the chosen container.
  • Sun: Advice about the selected container’s recorded light level and the plants growing in it.

Guidance while changing your layout

In Sun Map, Full sun, Part sun, or Shade describes the light level you’re applying. The Sun guidance below can show how that choice affects existing plants. Follow Sun Map for light levels and compass instructions.

While plotting, brief instructions explain the gesture or why a plant cannot fit in a particular spot. In Separate plants, Minimum size and Minimum shape show whether the selected squares meet the plant’s footprint requirements. Follow Manage several squares as one planting for grouping and separating.

When a planting is selected, its removal control appears below its guidance. Follow Move and remove plantings to clear it from the space and record what happened.

Choose companions while plotting

Companions are useful neighbors. They may attract helpful insects, provide support, cover soil, or make better use of the space. The guidance explains the relationship and any conditions or cautions to consider.

When Companions appears while plotting in a bed, tap its heading and turn on Highlight companions to locate related plants. Read the accompanying advice to understand how those plants may affect one another.

Find the plants mentioned in the Guide

Tap a bold plant name to select it. When the advice applies to several plantings of the same type, choose the one you want from the menu.

Press and hold the bold name to temporarily highlight the relevant planting or plantings in the visible bed or containers. Release to return to your previous view. This is useful when you only want to locate a plant without leaving the guidance you were reading.
