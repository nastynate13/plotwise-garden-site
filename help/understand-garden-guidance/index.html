Understand your garden’s guidance

Nov 12, 2026

3 min read

Schedule & guidance Guide Climate Sun Moisture Health Space & growth Bold plant names Highlight

Use the Guide to understand your planting choices and notice what may need attention as your bed or containers change.

The Guide follows what you are doing

With nothing selected, guidance looks across the bed or container group. Select a plant to focus on that planting. While adding plants, guidance helps you choose a space; after planting, it can focus on establishment, support, weather, or readiness.

Cards appear when the app has relevant guidance. You will not always see every category, and a short Guide does not mean your garden is incomplete.

Start with Today when you want the wider view

Almanac → Today gathers attention items, upcoming plantings, active schedule windows, and recent journal entries across your garden. Open a relevant planting or growing space for its detailed Guide. An empty attention section is not a health inspection; it means there are no current items to show from the information Plotwise has.

What shapes the advice?

Depending on the card, Plotwise uses the plant and selected variety, its footprint or container capacity, recorded sunlight, garden location, planting method, saved date, and whether it is planned or planted. Weather guidance also depends on available weather information.

A planned date does not mean the plant has started growing. Confirming Plant gives the Guide an actual date to use. Likewise, an estimated window passing does not tell the app that the plant has finished or been removed.

Read each card for a different question

  • Layout: How much space is occupied, and what is still available?
  • Sun: Does the recorded light suit the plants, and might taller neighbors shade them?
  • Space & growth: What does mature size, spreading or climbing growth, or support mean for this planting?
  • Climate: How might seasonal heat, cold, and planting timing affect it?
  • Moisture: Do recent or forecast conditions call for a closer look at the soil or plant?
  • Health: Which pests, diseases, disorders, or symptoms are worth knowing about?
  • Succession: What should you consider before reusing the space?

Tap a card’s heading for its explanation. A health guide is a reference to help you investigate; its appearance does not mean Plotwise has detected a disease in your garden.

Find the plants mentioned in a card

Tap a bold plant name to select it. When the advice applies to several plantings of the same type, choose the one you want from the menu.

Press and hold the bold name to temporarily highlight the relevant planting or plantings in the visible bed or containers. Release to return to your previous view. This is useful when you only want to locate a plant without leaving the guidance you were reading.

When advice does not fit what you see

Check the recorded details first: correct location, sun level, variety, method, date, and planted state. A regional planting window is an estimate, and the app cannot measure your soil or see a building’s shadow from the layout alone.

Follow Explore health guides or Explore supports when offered for more detail. These links open related catalog content; they do not record a problem, purchase supplies, or change your planting.
