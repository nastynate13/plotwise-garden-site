Grow in containers

Nov 12, 2026

3 min read

Organize your pots, grow bags, and planters in a container group.

A group is a collection of containers

A container group might represent your patio, balcony, or a collection of pots beside a bed. The group helps you view and manage them together. Each container still has its own capacity and sunlight setting.

Set up the group

  1. In My Garden, tap plus (+) and choose Containers in Garden Setup.
  2. Select the garden’s location and give the group a recognizable name.
  3. Add containers with types and sizes that represent the ones you use.
  4. Review the containers and finish setup.

Choose a meaningful name for each container when you need to tell similar pots apart—for example, Blue pot or Long balcony planter.

What does a space mean?

A container can offer one or more planting spaces. Each space lets you add a planting to that container. Some plants need more of a container’s capacity than others.

Choose the container and an available space when adding a plant. Suitability depends on the plant and container. A small empty pot does not necessarily have enough soil volume or growing room for a large plant.

Where a container has several spaces, a plant’s location can include its space number. This helps distinguish plantings that share the same container.

Plot, plan, and plant

Choosing a space for a plant makes it Plotted. Save its method and date in Edit plan to make it Planned. Use Plant to record when it actually begins growing there.

Use the group’s Guide, Schedule, and Journal to see its plantings together. Select a container or planting to focus on it. The Guide can take container size and sun exposure into account alongside timing and other available information.

Adjust the group as it changes

Open Edit group to change the group name, add containers, or update individual containers.

  • Tap a container’s name to rename it.
  • Use its sun control to record its light level.
  • Reorder containers to make the display easier to scan.
  • Swipe a container to remove it, and review the confirmation before continuing.

Removing an occupied container also clears its plantings from the group. Adding and removing containers contributes to its recorded history.
