Choose varieties and use the catalogs

Nov 12, 2026

3 min read

Catalogs & photo identification Varieties Custom variety Supplier Seeds Garden Health Garden Supplies Growing details

Choose what you are growing, understand the difference a variety can make, and find related health and supply guides without leaving the garden’s context behind.

Three catalogs, three purposes

Plants helps you explore plants and varieties, read growing information, and choose what to add to a bed or container.

Garden Health covers pests, diseases, beneficial garden life, and other conditions to investigate. Its guides explain identifying features and practical responses; they do not diagnose a planting just because you opened them.

Garden Supplies explains items used to grow and care for plants, including support options. Follow the retailer links when you want to investigate a product further.

Open these from Search → Catalog. You can browse categories without typing, or search for a name. Searching for a plant can also surface related health and supply guides.

Choose a variety

When adding a plant, open its variety menu. Choose Browse all to read variety summaries and see supplier links, or open an individual variety for its growing details.

Use Choose [variety] when you want to use it for the planting. Simply reading a supplier page does not select the variety or buy seeds.

You can also explore varieties from the plant’s information page. When browsing without a planting selected, changing the variety previews its information; it does not change existing plants in your garden.

What changes with a variety?

Available variety information can refine details such as maturity, size, spacing, sunlight, or support. The app uses supported growing details where available and falls back to general plant guidance for missing information.

Supplier maturity figures may describe different starting points, such as sowing or transplanting. They are not all interchangeable countdowns from today. Review the source and method along with the number.

A variety can be updated for a planned planting. Once the planting is marked as planted, the recorded variety is locked for that planting.

Add a variety you already own

  1. Open the plant’s variety menu and choose Add.
  2. Enter the variety name from the seed packet or plant label.
  3. Optionally tap Find growing details.
  4. Review a matching supplier listing and its details before selecting it.
  5. Save the variety, or choose it for the planting when adding from that flow.

The current lookup searches Burpee and Baker Creek. A missing result does not mean the variety is invalid: you can save its name without supplier details and use the general plant guidance. Saved custom varieties appear under Your varieties for that plant.

Follow guidance into a catalog

Links such as Explore supports and Explore health guides show relevant choices for the planting you were viewing. Check the related section’s heading so you know which plant it concerns; you can still browse the rest of the catalog.
