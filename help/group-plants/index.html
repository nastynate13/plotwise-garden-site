Manage several squares as one planting

Nov 12, 2026

Nathan Morgan

3 min read

Grouping Separate plants Succession

Manage several squares of the same plant as one planting, with a shared plan, planting date, removal, and succession plan.

When grouping helps

If you’re planting several squares of garlic on the same day, grouping lets you plan and plant them together. With grouping off, each plant you place can be managed independently.

Grouped plants share one identity in the schedule, journal, and search, rather than becoming a separate planting for every square.

1. Turn on Group plants

Open a bed, tap the plus (+) button, and choose a plant. While placing it, tap Group plants, shown with a four-square grid icon.

The icon is filled and colored when grouping is on. An outline indicates that it is off. After you’ve used the control, it can appear as an icon in the header above the guidance cards.

Grouping applies to the plants you add during this placement session. It does not merge plantings you saved earlier.

2. Add the squares you want to manage together

Place the same plant and variety in the squares you want included. Grouped areas do not have to touch. For example, you can group flowers along different edges of a bed.

Each plant still needs its minimum size and shape. A plant that requires a 2 × 2 block needs complete blocks; four scattered squares are not equivalent.

Tap the checkmark (✓) to finish. The new group is now a single planting.

3. Plan and plant the group

Tap a square belonging to the group to select the planting. Use Edit plan to choose one method and date for the group, then Plant to confirm when it begins growing.

Removing this planting applies to the group. A succession plan also uses the group’s space, subject to the next plant’s footprint requirements.

If you want one part to have a different date, removal, or succession, separate that part first.

4. Separate part of a planting later

  1. Select the planting in your bed.
  2. Tap Separate plants, shown with the overlapping-squares icon. After you’ve used it, the control can appear in the header.
  3. Tap the squares you want to manage together as a separate planting.
  4. Check Minimum size and Minimum shape below the bed. Both the selection and the part left behind must have a valid footprint. Existing successions can also affect whether a split is possible.
  5. Tap the checkmark (✓) to save, or X to cancel.

Your selected squares become one new planting. They keep the current plan, which you can then change independently.

Common questions

Can I group different plants or varieties?

No. Grouping is for the same plant and variety. Use separate plantings when they need to be managed differently.

Can I select every square when separating?

No. Leave a valid planting behind. For garlic, that can be one square. A larger plant needs its full minimum footprint in both parts.

Is grouping available for containers?

This grouping control is for bed squares. Container plantings are managed through their available container spaces.
