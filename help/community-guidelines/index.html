Community guidelines

Nov 12, 2026

Nathan Morgan

1 min read

Community

Share gardening experience, ask questions, and help others learn. Be kind when experiences or advice differ.

Treat other gardeners with respect

No harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content, or graphic violence.

Keep Community useful

No spam, scams, or repeated unwanted promotion.

Share responsibly

Share only photos and text you have the right to share. Do not post someone else’s private details.

Be clear about gardening advice

Describe uncertainty in advice. Follow product labels and local rules for pesticides and other garden treatments.

Safety checks and moderation

Posts, replies, and public profiles undergo automated safety checks. A check does not verify gardening advice or guarantee that content is appropriate.

Report concerning content or block a gardener from the app. A moderator can remove content and pause an account’s posting.

Questions or concerns

Contact plotwisegarden@gmail.com about a decision or concern. Our Support page explains how to report content and get help.
