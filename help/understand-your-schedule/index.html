Understand planting dates and your schedule

Nov 12, 2026

3 min read

Schedule & guidance Dates Seed Transplant Harvest Ready soon Check growth No estimate Planned Planted

See planting dates and estimated lifecycle windows together on the schedule.

Three different kinds of date

A planned planting date is when you intend to sow, transplant, or start the plant in its space. Save it with a planting method in Edit plan. You can reserve space before deciding on either.

The actual planting date records what happened. Use Plant when the plant begins growing in that bed or container, and enter the actual date—even if it differs from the plan.

An estimated window describes what may come next: production, harvest, establishment, or a time to clear a cover crop. The kind of window depends on the plant. It isn’t always a harvest window.

For a transplant, the planting date is when the young plant moves into this growing space. It is not the date its seed was started elsewhere. Seed and transplant estimates can differ.

Open the right schedule

Use Almanac → Schedule for the whole garden. Inside a bed or container group, choose Schedule to focus on that space.

Scroll the chart to explore dates and plantings. Use Full-screen chart for more room. Sort by Window start, Planting date, or Alphabetical. Successions stay with the planting they follow, in planting order.

The Overview separates Plotted, Planned, and Planted plantings. Tap a count to choose a planting. Active and Ready soon show plantings with current or approaching estimated windows.

Filters such as Planting soon, Ready soon, and Window active narrow the chart. Ready soon refers to an approaching estimated window.

Check your plants as each window begins

The estimate uses the planting method, date, and available plant or variety information. Seasonal guidance also uses the garden’s location. Weather, growing conditions, and the size of a transplant can still change how the plant develops.

When a window begins, check how the plant is developing. A tomato’s first ripe fruit and a carrot’s usable root size are different milestones.

When dates or conditions change

Adjust an unplanted plan in Edit plan. Confirm the actual date through Plant when you plant. Read any timing or climate message alongside the estimate; it can explain why a particular date might be problematic.

An estimated window ending does not remove a plant or free its space. Keep a productive plant as long as you want, and record removal when it actually happens. Plan the next planting around when you expect to clear the space.

If a planting is missing

A Plotted planting needs a saved method and date to appear on the dated chart. Find it in the Plotted list and use Edit plan.
