Create your first bed

Nov 12, 2026

Nathan Morgan

3 min read

Getting started Bed setup Location Sunlight

Create a bed that matches your growing space, choose its location, and map the sunlight before adding your first plants.

1. Start in My Garden

Open My Garden and tap the plus (+) button. In Garden Setup, choose Bed, then continue.

A bed is a rectangular growing area shown as a square-foot grid. For pots, grow bags, window boxes, or planters, choose Containers instead.

2. Choose where you’re growing

Use your current location, or search for the location of your garden. Tap a search result to select it before continuing. Typing a place name alone does not select it.

You can also give the location a familiar name, such as Home or Community Garden. Plotwise uses the location for climate and weather guidance.

3. Name and size your bed

Enter a name you’ll recognize, such as Kitchen Bed. Choose a listed size or use Custom size to set the width and length in feet.

Each square represents one square foot. A 4 × 8-foot bed has 32 squares. Check the dimensions before creating the bed; they define the space you’ll plan within.

4. Map the sunlight

Choose whether to open the Sun Map next, then tap Create Bed.

Every square starts as full sun. In the sun editor, choose a light level and mark the areas that receive less direct sunlight:

  • Full sun: 6 or more hours of direct light.
  • Part sun: roughly 3 to 6 hours.
  • Shade: less than 3 hours.

Use what you observe in the real bed. You can skip this step and return to it later; the full-sun default is a starting point, not a measurement of your garden.

5. Add your first plants

Open the bed and tap the plus (+) button to browse plants. Choose a plant, add it to the grid, and tap the checkmark (✓) when you’re done placing it.

Plants that need one square can be added by tapping squares. For plants with a larger minimum footprint, long press and drag across enough squares. The plant’s catalog card shows the space it needs.

Placing a plant reserves space in your plan. You can decide on its method and planting date afterward.

Common questions

Can I plan more than one bed?

Yes. Return to My Garden and use the plus button to add another bed or container group. Growing spaces are organized by location.

Do I need to finish planning everything now?

No. Start with a few plants and return whenever you’re ready. You can arrange your bed before choosing planting dates.

Why can’t I continue after entering my location?

Choose one of the results below the search field so Plotwise has a selected location.
