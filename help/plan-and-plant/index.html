Plan a planting and mark it as planted

Nov 12, 2026

4 min read

Reserve a place for a plant, choose how and when you intend to grow it, then record the actual planting date when it begins growing in your bed or container.

Planned and planted

Planned means you’ve reserved space for a plant. You can arrange plants first and choose their planting methods and dates later.

Planted means you’ve confirmed that the plant is growing in that space. This records the actual planting date.

1. Choose a plant and variety

Open a bed or container group and tap the plus (+) button. Browse the plant catalog or search for a plant.

If you know the variety you’re growing, choose it from the plant’s variety options. You can browse more varieties or add a custom variety there too. You can also start with the general plant.

2. Reserve its space

In a bed, tap squares to add a single-square plant. For a plant with a larger minimum footprint, long press and drag across enough squares. In a container group, choose an available space in a suitable container.

Tap the checkmark (✓) when you’re done. This saves the placement in your garden plan.

Want several squares to share one plan? Turn on Group plants while placing them in a bed. Learn how grouping works.

3. Save the planting method and date

Select the plant in your bed or container, then tap Edit plan in its details.

Choose the planting method you intend to use and the date you expect to plant in that space. The available methods depend on the plant. For example, garlic can use Clove, while other plants may offer Seed or Transplant.

For a transplant, use the date the young plant will move into this bed or container, rather than the date its seed was started elsewhere.

Plotwise may offer a suggested date. Review it along with any timing guidance, make adjustments, and tap Save. The suggestion is not a saved plan until you save it.

You can return to Edit plan if your plans change.

4. Confirm when you plant

Once you’ve saved the plan, the Plant button becomes available. When you sow, transplant, or otherwise start the plant in its space:

  1. Select the planting and tap Plant.
  2. Set Planted on to the actual date it began growing there.
  3. Optionally turn on Add journal entry to include a note or photo.
  4. Tap the Plant button at the bottom to confirm.

If it was already planted, enter the actual earlier date.

5. Follow it on the schedule

Open Schedule for that growing space, or use Almanac → Schedule to see plantings across your garden.

Planting dates and estimated growing or harvest windows help you see what’s ahead. These are estimates: variety, weather, planting method, and the plant’s actual progress can change when it’s ready.

Common questions

Why is the Plant button disabled?

Save a planting method and date in Edit plan first. You can then confirm the actual date using Plant.

Can I leave a plant without a date for now?

Yes. Its space stays reserved in the plan. Add the method and date when you’re ready for timing guidance.

Does reaching the planned date mark it as planted?

No. Use Plant to confirm what happened in your garden.
